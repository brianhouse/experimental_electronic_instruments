- this is a studio course: a space for collating inspirations and conducting experiments

- it is an intermediate follow up to intro to sound art, along one particular branch of what sound art can be. so on some level, I am assuming knowledge intro to sound art. if you didn't take that course, that's alright, I'll catch people up as we go.

- because regardless, this will be an informal course, because I'm not sure what it is yet, so this an experiment. not even sure what to call this class.

- but we're primarily going to do two things. the first is to learn how to do sound synthesis using the visual programming language Pd. (simmilar to Max)

- the second is to then use that knowledge to embed sound engines on a microcontroller (the Daisy Seed) and to build a whole "instrument" around it. 

- we're going to be working together on this, I'm new at Pd, and have never used the seed before. so we'll do our best.

- if you do the work, you will get an A, so we can relieve ourselves of that particular anxiety. I want people to "show up".

- so this is not exactly synthesizer design in the sense of all-analog electronics, and it's not exactly instrument design in terms of the physics of sound in space.

- we're in this in-between realm of constructing interfaces. and that idea of "interface" to me is really essential. we are surrounded by interfaces, many of which we take for granted. but there is a lot going on when we touch a screen or turn a handle. and that's what I want to explore conceptually. 

- a question of design, but also one of art.
