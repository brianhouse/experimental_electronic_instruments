# Final project

Create an electronic instrument of your own design. Equal attention should be paid to nature of the sound produced by the instrument and the physical design of its interface. Your choices should be intentional, personal, and original. Your instrument must have a name and a short statement that articulates your approach, and you should be able to justify your choices in critique.


### Proposal

Prior to starting the project, you will turn in a proposal, which should be organized in slides and exported to PDF format. It should include:
- your preliminary concept and any relevant illustrative material
- how it relates to class discussion
- how it relates to your studio practice / personal interests
- how you will build it and what materials and tools you are planning on using
- any technical or conceptual questions that you will need to solve
- questions that you have for the class about your instrument

Upload this proposal to Moodle under the section for this project. We will discuss these proposals in class.


### Deliverables

You will present your instrument in class, giving a short performance to demonstrate its features.

In addition, you will produce a documentation video from 2–3 minutes long. You may use your phone, but film in **landscape**, [use AE/AF Lock](https://improvephotography.com/53127/what-is-ae-af-lock-on-an-iphone-and-how-does-that-help-my-pictures/), and use a tripod. You are encouraged to make use of the photo both in the back of 303. You do not need titles or a spoken explanation—just demonstrate your instrument doing its thing. Audio should be recorded live with a Tascam recorder and synced with the video afterwards.
